              💨  Dodge. Dip. Shoot The Shit.

The game is EZ..
Shoot the shit, and avoid the assholes.
(Kinda like real life.)
The player who shoots the most shit, is the Supreme Shit Shooter!
(We call him 007.. 0 work, 0 skills, 7 smoke breaks..)

CONTROLS:
- W/A/S/D — Move your ship
- Spacebar — Shoot / Fire Milk Beam
- Left Alt — Brake
- Hold ` (or Y / RB on a controller) — Hide the HUD, so no poop can hide behind it

Increase your score by shooting poops, and collecting power-ups!

THE DEUCE DETECTOR:
The meter at the bottom of the screen counts every poop left on the board.
Flush them ALL and The Inflation Station shows up with an Endowment for you.
The closer you get to zero, the louder it gets. The last 10 are the hardest. Clench.

POOP STREAKS:
Every poop you shoot in a row builds your Streak.
20 in a row = x2 poop points. 50 = x3. 90 = x4.
Pop a butt and the Streak is over. Missing doesn't count — only butts break it.

THE RAMPAGE:
Once you've been Endowed, the shit's outta the way... and it's time to play.
Enemy ships keep coming for the rest of the galaxy, and the deeper the galaxy,
the more of them can show up at once. They're scared of THE WORM though.
Every bullet that DOES NOT shoot a poop will increase your "Spice Level".
The "Spice Level" determines how "Spicy" the boss will be when it spawns.
The higher your Spice Level, the more health the boss spawns with — so keep it low!
The boss comes for you when the "Boss Sauce" bottle is full and the cap blows off.
Every Butt that's busted adds a squirt to the "Boss Sauce" bottle.
This is because clapping cheeks attracts the Boss.

The Boss is Sir Reginald Shaftsworth III. Or Big Bertha. Or Uncle Nutsy.
Watch the label on the bottle — whoever's on it is coming next.
They ALL want to fuck your ass.
Run and shoot them till they die.
When a Boss dies, it destroys all poops/butts on screen,
and then all speeds/multipliers increase by 15%.
You are awarded points for any poops destroyed by a Boss's death explosion.
(Yes, you can have more than one Boss at the party. This is not fun.)

Your highest score is saved automatically between sessions. Can you beat it?

THE COSMIC CHOCOLATE CHUTE:
Beat the worm and fly into the rainbow hole to warp to a new galaxy.
Clear the board fast and you'll earn a Warp Bonus on the way through.

Use power-ups to help shoot the shit, and stay alive.
The Condom wraps your cockpit in a protective shield that allows you to take one additional hit.
If you collect a Condom while actively wearing one, you will receive 50 points!
(this is equivalent to shooting 50 poops.. HUGE)

The Zinc Pill gives your cockpit the "Milk Beam"!
While using the Milk Beam, spice level cannot increase;
and any object destroyed by the Milk Beam decreases Spice Level.
(butts destroyed by the Milk Beam will still fill the "Boss Sauce" bottle so be careful!)
Hold Spacebar (shoot keybind) to constantly spray your Milk Beam.
The Milk Beam lasts for 15 seconds.
Collecting another Zinc Pill while Milk Beam is active will simply refresh the timer.

The DP Soda instantly recharges your thrusters, and let's you shoot two projectiles at once,
for a double dose of penetration. While DP Soda is active, you may use your thrusters at no cost,
for maximum Thrusting Power! 15 seconds of maximum Thrusting Power, anyway..

TIPS:
- Keep your spice level low.
- Try to keep Bosses to a minimum (defeating them makes the game harder).
- Spam shooting may not be ideal, unless you have the Milk Beam!
